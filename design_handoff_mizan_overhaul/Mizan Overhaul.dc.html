Where turn 2 is harder than turn 1

Uncertainty became a shape. The hollow dashed box is spending Mizan believes exists but hasn't read. It is never filled with an estimate — that's determinism you can see rather than a paragraph promising it.

The scale weighs the promise, not the spending. Actually saved vs what you agreed. It tilts by the gap and goes level when you hit 25% — one instrument you learn to read in a second.

Statements have a cadence. Catch up knows the HSBC lands on the 3rd, keeps the password, tells you what will change when it arrives — and a forward-to address removes the app from the loop entirely.

A Sunday notification. No weekly ritual survives without a trigger. It states the payoff and the cost up front: “fully measured now · 6 merchants · 3 minutes”.

Sorting handles the cases that stall people — a card payment that isn't spending, and one charge with two purposes. Both are asked as plain questions and remembered as rules, not amounts.

Settling is an action now. “Mark settled” writes a transfer to the ledger and never edits a closed month.
